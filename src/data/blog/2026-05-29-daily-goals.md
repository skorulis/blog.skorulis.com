I'm trying a small habit: write down what I want to accomplish today, then work from that list. For May 29, the main goal is clear. I need to figure out how to get more people to download [Straight Facts](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/straight-facts-daily-trivia/id6767988129), my free daily trivia app. Right now I'm at about two daily users. That is not really a trend so much as a very quiet room.

## Four directions to explore

I listed a few ways the number might move:

1. **App Store Optimization (ASO)** — Figure out how to pull more traffic straight from the App Store listing.
2. **More platforms** — Ship the app beyond iOS.
3. **Find channels** — Look for places where I can share the app and actually reach people who might try it.
4. **Build channels** — Create ways to share content outside the app, not only links to the download page.

These overlap. They are different levers, and I do not yet know which one responds fastest.

## Time to make a mac version

Option 2 is probably not the shortest path to more downloads. ASO, outreach, and content distribution would likely surface users sooner than another build target. I still keep gravitating toward the Mac version today.

Some of that is temperament. Porting to macOS lets me stay in code instead of marketing, which I am still learning how to do. There are also a few practical reasons it does not feel like a detour:

- **New territory.** I have never shipped a Mac app. It would be a useful test of how much of my SwiftUI code carries over between platforms.
- **A quieter store.** The Mac App Store is far less crowded than iOS. Standing out might be easier, even if the audience is smaller.
- **Another baseline.** A second platform adds a data point for analytics, which helps when I am guessing what is working on iOS alone.
- **Content from the desktop.** A Mac build opens up ways to generate shareable material (screenshots, short clips, and similar) for channels like Instagram, without treating the phone app as the only surface.

So today's plan is less about locking in the perfect growth strategy and more about making progress on the problem. ASO, distribution, and content channels stay on the list. For now, I want to see how far I can get on a Mac build and whether that teaches me anything useful about the other three.
