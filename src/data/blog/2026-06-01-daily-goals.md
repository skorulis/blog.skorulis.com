Another day, another list. The habit from [May 29](/2026/05/29/daily-goals) still feels useful: write down what matters today, then work from that. June 1 has a clearer shape than last time. Less exploring directions, more shipping what is already in motion.

## Straight Facts 1.5

The main goal is to get version 1.5 of [Straight Facts](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/straight-facts-daily-trivia/id6767988129) ready for release. The headline change is the share system. I am overhauling how sharing works in the app, with the hope that sharing becomes a real growth lever rather than an afterthought.

That fits the broader problem I have been circling: downloads are still flat, and I need more ways for existing users to bring in new ones. A better share flow will not fix discovery on its own, but it is a concrete step toward treating distribution as part of the product, not an afterthought.

This version will also drop the age range from 15+ to 9+ which will provide another data point for ASO.

## Posting every day

Last week I build a video export pipeline for social media. The plumbing is in place now, so the next phase is actually using it. I am aiming for one new post each day on Instagram and TikTok.

The idea is that the channels might feed each other: a clip on one platform points people toward the other, and both point back toward the app. I am not wildly optimistic. Right now they are all ghost towns, so cross-promotion might not move the needle much. Still, empty channels do not get less empty if I never post. Consistency is the experiment.

## Users before features

There are a few other features I could pick up but those can wait until after this release. Building more for an audience of two daily users is hard to justify when the bottleneck is clearly on the distribution side.
