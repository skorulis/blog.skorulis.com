[Yesterday](/2026/06/01/daily-goals) was about shipping Straight Facts 1.5 and keeping the social channels fed. Today the focus shifts outward: the sites around the app, and where new users might actually come from.

## Rebuilding skorulis.com

I have decided to rebuild my [skorulis.com](https://skorulis.com) home page. The hope is that a fresher site might pull a bit more traffic over time. These days that kind of refresh is mostly a quick AI prompt and some cleanup, which makes it easy to justify trying.

The [Straight Facts](https://www.skorulis.com/straightfacts/) landing page is in the same boat: very bare bones, mostly straight facts about the app. It could use a facelift to match whatever I do on the main site.

I also thought about adding more written content to the site, but I am short on new ideas at the moment. That can wait until something worth saying shows up.

## Sydney Beer Guide

I considered reviving an old project called Sydney Beer Guide. The code appears to be gone for good, and I am not sure the idea is worth rebuilding from scratch. For now it stays on the shelf.

## Distribution next

I have not tried submitting Straight Facts to Product Hunt or similar launch sites yet. That might be the next avenue, especially if I can find niche directories that fit a daily trivia app better than a general-purpose feed. Worth a short list and a few submissions before building more features nobody has seen.
