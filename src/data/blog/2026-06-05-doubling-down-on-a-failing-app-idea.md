Lately I have been putting real effort into [Fact Snack](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fact-snack-daily-trivia/id6767988129). Not in the form of flashy new features, but in polishing the UI and trying to figure out how to get people interested in the first place.

## The numbers are not great

To be blunt, it is doing absolutely awful. Three weeks in and I am sitting on about 12 downloads, almost no organic traffic, and no obvious place to market it. I have poked at social channels, thought about launch sites, and kept the landing page honest. None of it has moved the needle yet.

I still think the app is well built. In my (admittedly biased) view, it is better than most of what is already out there for daily trivia. I still believe there is a space for something calm and source-backed, even if I have not figured out how to sell that idea yet.

## Same app, different punchline

So today I am taking the same concept and UI and turning it into a daily joke app.

The reason is not that I have fallen out of love with facts. It is purely about getting more data. I do not know why Fact Snack is failing to attract views, and running a second app in parallel means I can collect twice the signal from the same amount of effort.

## Why this is not as crazy as it sounds

The lift is smaller than it sounds. I am expecting to have the new app ready for App Store review in two or three days, and a fair chunk of the work will improve the shared codebase for the original app anyway. Shared UI, shared infrastructure, one more experiment on top of what already exists.

Then I can compare outcomes. Does the joke app hit the same wall, or does it surface something I got wrong on the facts side? Maybe the category is the problem. Maybe the pitch is. Maybe distribution is just hard for both and I need a completely different channel. I will not know until I have two data points instead of one.

It also opens up room for cross-promotion and gives me a slightly bigger marketing surface. Two apps with a similar vibe might be easier to talk about than one lonely trivia app that nobody has heard of.

## The bet

This is not a pivot so much as a deliberate double-down. I am still polishing Fact Snack. I still believe in it. I am just adding a sibling app to learn faster, reuse what I have already built, and see whether the problem is the product, the positioning, or something I have not spotted yet.

If nothing else, I will have a better answer than "I do not know why this is not working." That alone feels worth a few days of work.
