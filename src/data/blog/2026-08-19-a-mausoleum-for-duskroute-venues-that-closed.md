I ran into a problem on [DuskRoute](https://duskroute.com) that was always going to happen sooner or later. A venue I had collected specials for had gone out of business. It was still showing up in search results, and I did not want people turning up to a door that no longer opened.

## Delete it and move on?

The easiest option would have been to delete the data. The catch is that it was already indexed in Google, so wiping it would have left dead links. I was not expecting any real traffic from those pages, but I still did not like the idea.

## A resting place instead

So instead of dropping closed venues completely, I built [the mausoleum](https://duskroute.com/mausoleum). It is a place to keep the historical deals. Over time it should become a small record of what happy hours used to look like, for anyone who wants to look back fondly at the places they used to go.
