Amy and I are spending three weeks heading north through Vietnam, from the 7th to the 29th of September. The route starts in Hoi An and works its way up through Da Nang, Phong Nha and Sapa, then on to Ninh Binh, Cat Ba Island and a final few days in Hanoi. The plan was street food, caves, rice paddies and cheap beer. Rain has been a more reliable companion than any of those.

This page is the index for the trip. I will add links here as each stop gets written up.

## Stops so far

- [A day in Hoi An](/2026/09/09/a-day-in-hoi-an/). Coconut boats, lanterns on the river, and a fresh beer that cost 15k VND.
- [Da Nang: Steps, Caves, and Craft Beer](/2026/09/10/da-nang-steps-caves-and-craft-beer/). Marble Mountains, Linh Ung Pagoda, packed markets, and a jacket potato that fixed everything.
- [Phong Nha: Caves and Rain](/2026/09/11/phong-nha-caves-rain-and-cold-beer/). A jungle trek in the wet, Paradise Cave, a rising river, and a bike ride to the Pub with Cold Beer.
- [Hanoi: 1 day stopover](/2026/09/15/hanoi-1-day-stopover/). Banh mi, Hoan Kiem Lake in the rain, and a hunt for bia hoi between Phong Nha and Sapa.
- [Sapa: Rain, Mud and Fansipan](/2026/09/16/sapa-rain-mud-and-fansipan/). Cat Cat Village, muddy rice paddies, and a cable car above the clouds.
