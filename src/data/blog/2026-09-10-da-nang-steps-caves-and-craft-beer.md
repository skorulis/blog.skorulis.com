We went from Hoi An to Da Nang at around midday on the 9th of September, taking a hotel transfer since it is an easy option for the 45-minute drive. Just as the rain really started to come down.

We were too early to check in so went for a walk along the beach. The sign says it is rated 6th among all world beaches, but I think Aussie beaches have it beat. Da Nang is such a huge difference from Hoi An. It is a city nestled alongside beach resorts.

As an afternoon activity we went to the Marble Mountains. So many steps. Reminded me of Minecraft with how many things had been built inside the caves on top of a mountain.

<img src="/images/da-nang/marble-mountains.jpg" alt="Looking down stone steps into a cave chamber with shrines at the Marble Mountains" class="m-0 w-full border border-border" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

Stopped for two craft beers at Bot Craft Bar and then headed to Pho Hòa for a beef noodle soup dinner with a side of morning glory, which was 280k VND (~$15 AUD).

<img src="/images/da-nang/bot-craft-bar.jpg" alt="Bot Craft Beer lit up at night with a mural visible through the window" class="m-0 w-full border border-border" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

We headed back to the hotel via the supermarket to grab some beers and walked outside to a huge downpour. Luckily there were tables outside, so we joined the others enjoying a beer waiting for the rain to die down.

Had yet another banh mi for breakfast which came with some iced tea, which we enjoyed on tiny seats. A great deal at only 90k VND (~$5 AUD).

From there we went to Linh Ung Pagoda. It is quite a nice drive along the beachside past the fishing boats. That was really well maintained and beautiful, worth visiting and free.

<img src="/images/da-nang/linh-ung-pagoda.jpg" alt="Standing in the courtyard at Linh Ung Pagoda with the tall white Lady Buddha statue behind" class="m-0 w-full border border-border" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

We got a Grab car back about halfway and then did an hour walk along the beach. Felt like an accomplishment in the heat.

After that we went to the Con Market, arriving just as a downpour started. It was a twisting labyrinth of stalls with barely enough space to walk between. From there we walked down towards the Han Market, stopping off for a fried rice lunch with a fantastic mango smoothie for 170k VND (~$9 AUD). The Han Market was more organised but absolutely packed full of shoppers, so once again it was hard to navigate.

<img src="/images/da-nang/han-market.jpg" alt="Crowded aisles and stalls packed with goods inside Han Market in Da Nang" class="m-0 w-full border border-border" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

After that we went back to the hotel and sat up at the rooftop pool and watched the rains slowly roll in. It was expected to get much worse in the coming days.

<img src="/images/da-nang/rooftop-pool.jpg" alt="Rain falling into the rooftop pool with a misty Da Nang skyline beyond the glass railing" class="m-0 w-full border border-border" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

By this time my stomach had started protesting heavily about my sudden change in diet, so we opted for a jacket potato with cheese and bacon for dinner. Only 150k VND (~$8 AUD), with magical healing qualities that made me feel much better.
