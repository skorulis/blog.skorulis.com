Amy and I are spending a month in Japan, from the 13th of October to the 12th of November. The route starts in Osaka and heads west to Hiroshima, back east through Nara and Nagoya, up into the mountains at Matsumoto and Nagano, and finishes with a final stretch in Tokyo. The plan is castles, temples, trains, supermarket sashimi and whatever the bottom shelf of the whisky aisle has to offer.

This page is the index for the trip. I will add links here as each stop gets written up.

## Stops so far

- [Osaka: Castles, Supermarket Sashimi and Cheap Whisky](/2026/10/13/osaka-castles-sashimi-and-cheap-whisky/). Osaka Castle, a day trip to Himeji, the Hoshi no Buranko bridge, Kobe, kushikatsu in Shinsekai and the bottom rung of Japanese whisky.
- [Hiroshima: Miyajima, Sake Street and Okonomiyaki](/2026/10/18/hiroshima-miyajima-sake-street-and-okonomiyaki/). Hiking Mt Misen on Miyajima, Hiroshima Castle, a tipsy afternoon on Saijo's sake street, okonomiyaki at Okonomimura and the Peace Memorial Museum.
